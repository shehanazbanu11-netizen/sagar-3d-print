About us

Sagar 3D Prints is a small India-based print shop making custom idols, home decor, desk accessories and gifts — every piece printed to order, layer by layer, rather than mass produced. Have an idea in mind? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll quote it for you.

Frequently asked questions

Shipping & returns

Shipping

Flat ₹80 shipping across India on every order. Orders are printed to order, so please allow 2–5 days for printing before dispatch.

Returns & refunds

Since every piece is custom made, we don't accept change-of-mind returns. If your order arrives damaged or defective, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours with a photo and we'll replace or refund it.

Order tracking

Use the "Track order" link at the top of the page to message us on WhatsApp with your order details, and we'll share the latest status.
